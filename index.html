-- about

Reliable numbers first, then the story behind them

The sector changes; the craft does not. Whether the data describes insurance policies, hospital waiting lists or bank customers, I start by asking what decision the work needs to support. That shapes which sources I bring in, how I model the data and which measures matter.

Most of my effort goes into making the data dependable: combining sources with SQL and Python, building dimensional models, and putting reconciliation checks in place so problems are caught before anything is published. When figures move unexpectedly, I trace them back through the source records and transformation logic until I can explain why.

I currently work at HLPartnership on mortgage, protection and general insurance data. Before that I spent two years at an NHS Foundation Trust and started my career at IndusInd Bank. I hold an MSc in Advanced Computer Science from the University of Liverpool and an ILM Level 5 in Leadership and Management.

-- process

How I build analytics, end to end

  1. RequirementsAgree the decision, the audience and the measures that matter.
  2. SourcesMap how each system records the same activity.
  3. TransformationCombine, reshape and validate data in SQL and Python.
  4. ModellingBuild star schemas and single, governed measure definitions.
  5. ReportingDesign dashboards around the questions people ask.
  6. ReconciliationCheck outputs against source before every release.
  7. ImprovementAutomate manual steps and investigate unexpected movements.
